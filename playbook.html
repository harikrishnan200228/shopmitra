ShopMitra: shop owner playbook

One page. Read it in 3 minutes. Keep it at your counter.

What is ShopMitra?

A trained person from your own area (your operator) uses AI tools to handle the computer and phone work of your shop, so you can focus on customers and stock.

What your operator does

DailyReplies to WhatsApp and Instagram messages using prices and answers you approved.
WeeklyMakes posts and offer posters, and sends you the plan for the week.
MonthlyUpdates your sales and stock sheet, reminds customers about pending payments, replies to Google reviews.

Fashion and textile shops: extra help

  • A WhatsApp catalogue of new arrivals: clear photos, price, sizes, colours.
  • Offer posters for Onam, Vishu, Eid, Christmas and the wedding season.
  • Ready replies for common questions: "Is this available in M?", "Price per metre?", "Do you deliver?"
  • A stock list by fabric, colour and metres left, so you know when to reorder.
  • For tailors and boutiques: an order sheet with measurements, delivery date and balance due.

What you do (about 10 minutes a day)

  • Give your price list and shop timings once. Tell the operator what you never want promised.
  • Send fresh photos of new stock.
  • Approve the weekly posts and any reply the operator is unsure about.

Price and trial

First 2 weeks: free. No payment, no commitment.

After that: Rs [your price] per month. Stop any month you want.

You stay in control

  • The operator uses only the prices and answers you approved.
  • Anything unusual (discount requests, complaints, large orders) goes to you first.
  • You can see every message the operator sent for you.
  • Your customer list and sales numbers stay yours.
