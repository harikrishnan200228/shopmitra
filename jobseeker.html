Jobseeker kit: earn from your own area

You do not need a degree or an office. You need a smartphone, patience and the habit of finishing small tasks.

Your main income: monthly shop care

Look after 5 to 10 shops. Example: 8 shops at Rs 3,000 a month is Rs 24,000, and about Rs 16,800 for you after the platform share. These are example numbers. Your real income depends on how many shops you win.

Skills to learn in 2 weeks

  • Using an AI assistant to write replies and posts in Malayalam and English
  • Canva for posters and product photos
  • WhatsApp Business: catalogue, quick replies, labels
  • A simple stock and billing sheet in Google Sheets
  • Talking to shop owners politely and quoting a price

Your first 14 days

  • Days 1 to 3: Learn the tools. Practise on your own made-up shop.
  • Days 4 to 7: Visit 10 shops. Offer a free 2-week trial and show a sample post.
  • Days 8 to 14: Run one real shop. Keep before-and-after screenshots with the owner's permission.

Rules that protect you and the shop

  • Never promise a price or discount the owner has not approved.
  • Do not share a shop's customer numbers with anyone.
  • Be careful of any "job" that asks you to pay money first. Real work does not charge you to start.
  • Keep a record of every task you did. It becomes your proof of experience for future jobs.
